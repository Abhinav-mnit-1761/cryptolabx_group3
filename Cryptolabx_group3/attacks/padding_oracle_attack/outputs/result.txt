--- 5. CRYPTANALYTIC EXPLANATION ---
Why modifying the previous ciphertext block affects the target block:
In AES-CBC mode, decryption follows the equation:
    P_i = D_K(C_i) ^ C_{i-1} = I_i ^ C_{i-1}
where D_K is AES decryption under secret key K, and I_i is the intermediate state.
When an attacker submits a crafted block C'_{i-1} with target block C_i,
the oracle decrypts C_i to I_i and XORs it with C'_{i-1}:
    P'_i = I_i ^ C'_{i-1}
Because XOR is a bitwise linear operation, the attacker has complete 1-to-1
control over P'_i. By observing whether PKCS#7 padding succeeds, the attacker
solves for I_i byte-by-byte from right to left without knowing the AES key.

--- 6. SECURITY RECOMMENDATIONS ---
1. Authenticated Encryption: Use AEAD ciphers such as AES-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305.
2. Encrypt-then-MAC: If CBC must be used, verify HMAC over (IV || Ciphertext) BEFORE decryption.
3. Constant-Time Verification: Avoid leaking padding validity differences in error messages or timing.
